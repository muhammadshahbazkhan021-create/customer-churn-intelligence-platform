
# Exploratory Data Analysis Report

## Key Findings
- **Baseline Churn Rate:** ~26.5% positive attrition across raw observations.
- **Contract Impact:** Month-to-month contracts demonstrate 42% churn, compared to under 3% for two-year commitments.
- **Tenure Risk Zone:** First 12 months constitute over 50% of total customer losses.
- **Service Stack Correlation:** Customers without streaming, security, or backup add-ons have higher attrition probabilities.
